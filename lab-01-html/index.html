Welcome to SEU Tech Event 2026

Welcome to the SEU Tech Event 2026, a technology-focused event organized for students who are interested in modern technology, programming, artificial intelligence, networking, and innovation.

The event will provide students with an opportunity to learn new skills, meet technology enthusiasts, participate in competitions, and share innovative ideas with other students.

Event Highlight: Learn, compete, and connect with other technology enthusiasts.

About the Event

SEU Tech Event 2026 is designed to encourage students to explore technology beyond their regular academic activities. The event focuses on practical knowledge, creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving.

Goals of the Event

  • Develop practical technology skills.
  • Encourage innovation and creativity.
  • Improve teamwork and communication skills.
  • Introduce students to modern technologies.
  • Provide networking opportunities.

Event Activities

  • Technical Sessions
    • Web Development Workshop
    • Artificial Intelligence Seminar
    • Cyber Security Discussion
  • Competitions
    • Programming Contest
    • Project Showcase

Participation Details

Students from different departments of Southeast University can participate in the event. Participants should complete the online registration form and provide accurate student information.

How to Participate

  1. Read the event information carefully.
  2. Select your preferred event date.
  3. Fill out the registration form.
  4. Submit your registration.
  5. Attend the event on the selected date.

For more information about Southeast University, visit the official SEU website .
